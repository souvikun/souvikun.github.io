A little context

I like understanding things by taking them apart, looking at the pieces, and trying to put them back together in a way that makes sense to me.

Electrical engineering is particularly interesting because it lives in the space between mathematics and physical reality. A diagram can become a circuit. An equation can become a measurement. An idea can become a machine.

This website is an extension of that same curiosity — a place to keep notes, write about interesting ideas, and experiment with things that probably didn't need to exist.
